Timing-Tap — Story Cards

Rebuilt to your brief: each card is a random mini-story (a death scene, a survival, a legend) — not a flat line — so it's genuinely reaction-worthy. A crisp one-tap tagline, the Stay Alive wordmark, and App Store + Play Store badges sit together at the bottom so every share is an install ad.

Anatomy: status chip + emoji up top — a punchy headline plus a one-sentence story in the middle — footer stack: tagline ("One tap saves you — or ends you") + "Stay Alive" wordmark + App Store & Play Store badges. Stories are drawn at random from each pool, so no two shares feel the same.

Loading state — before the card resolves
The story content is fetched on request (random pull; post-launch via remote config), so there's a brief moment between the run ending and the card being ready. This fills it — a branded, on-theme loader so the screen never sits blank.
